CAN BUDGET — IPHONE SETUP

This package is iPhone-ready as a PWA (Progressive Web App). It is not an App Store .ipa; a true native iOS build requires macOS/Xcode.

ON YOUR WINDOWS PC:
1. Open Command Prompt in this folder.
2. Run:
   python -m http.server 8012 --bind 0.0.0.0
3. Find your PC's local IPv4 address:
   ipconfig
   Look for IPv4 Address under your Wi-Fi/Ethernet adapter.
4. Make sure the iPhone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.
5. On iPhone Safari, visit:
   http://YOUR-PC-IP:8012
   Example: http://192.168.1.25:8012
6. Tap Share.
7. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
8. Name it "Can Budget" and tap Add.

IMPORTANT:
- Your Can Budget data is stored in the browser on the device. The iPhone and PC have separate local data.
- Do not clear Safari website data for Can Budget unless you intend to erase its local data.
- The service worker/offline mode requires HTTPS on iPhone. The app is still usable from the PC over your local network without HTTPS.
- This version does not connect to a bank yet.

If Windows Firewall asks whether Python can communicate on the network, allow it on Private networks.
